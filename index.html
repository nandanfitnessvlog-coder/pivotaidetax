File an Extension

Need more time to file? We can submit your federal extension request quickly and correctly through our secure workflow.

Who this is for

This add-on is ideal if you’re still waiting on documents (W-2/1099/K-1), have a complex situation, or simply need extra time to finalize your return.

What’s included

Preparation and electronic filing of your extension request
Confirmation once submitted
Portal checklist for any remaining items needed to complete your return

Important to know

An extension gives you more time to file, not necessarily more time to pay. If you may owe, you may still incur interest or penalties.
