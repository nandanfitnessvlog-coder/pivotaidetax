Tax Extensions

What does an extension do?

An extension gives you more time to file, not more time to pay. If you expect to owe, making an estimated payment by the original deadline can help reduce penalties and interest.

Personal Tax Extensions (Individuals)

We assist with personal extensions by preparing and filing the appropriate federal extension (and guiding you on state extensions when applicable). We can also help you estimate any payment due based on the documents you have available and prior-year information.

Personal extension support typically includes:

  • Federal extension filing support
  • State extension guidance (as needed)
  • Estimated payment guidance (if you may owe)

Business Tax Extensions (Entities)

We also support business extensions for many entity filings (e.g., partnerships, S-corps, C-corps). Business extensions may require a reasonable estimate of the year's activity, and in some cases, bookkeeping completeness impacts the quality of the estimate.

Business extension support typically includes:

  • Extension filing support for eligible entity returns
  • Estimated payment guidance (if applicable)
  • Document checklist and timeline to finalize the return after extension filing
